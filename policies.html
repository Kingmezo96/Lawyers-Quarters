Editorial standards and privacy

Our editorial purpose

Lawyers’ Quarters covers law, professional practice, public affairs and culture. Henry Barnabas Ehi, MICA is identified as Editor-in-Chief. Ownership: Lawyers Quarters. Editorial contact: Lawyers Quarters.

Sources and content labels

News, analysis, opinion and sponsored material should be clearly distinguished. Legal conclusions require primary sources and editorial review. Sample material is not a verified legal finding. Views expressed by contributors are their own; publication does not imply NBA endorsement.

Corrections and complaints

Material corrections should identify the change and preserve the original publication date. To request a correction, provide the article title, disputed passage and supporting evidence. Editorial and complaints contact: Lawyers Quarters. Email and telephone details are not currently listed.

Conflicts and contributions

Contributors should disclose relevant personal, professional and commercial interests and provide source references and permission for submitted media. Submissions require editorial approval before publication.

Privacy and subscriptions

The newsletter preview is not connected to a delivery service. It does not submit your email or create a subscription. A production newsletter will need clear consent, frequency choices and an unsubscribe route.

Reading-list preferences may be stored in your browser. You can clear them using your browser’s site-data controls. Hosting providers may process routine request information. No new analytics or advertising tracker has been added in this update.

General information

Content provides general information, not advice on a particular matter. Confirm applicable law and obtain qualified advice before acting.
